4,312 files in my Downloads folder. That was Saturday morning's count.

So I wrote the dumbest useful script of my year. Forty lines of Python. It watches Downloads and moves things where they belong - PDFs to Documents, screenshots to a dated folder, installers to a quarantine I empty monthly.

Two hours to write. Half of that was a fight with macOS file events firing twice (if you know, you know).

Fourteen days later the folder is empty and I've stopped thinking about it. Which turned out to be the real win. Not the minutes saved - the attention.

Anyway. If your Downloads folder is currently a landfill, consider this your sign.
